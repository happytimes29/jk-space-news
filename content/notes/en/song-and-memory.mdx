When I opened Qishui Music today, the app started playing a random song. I heard a melody that felt familiar and unfamiliar at the same time. I usually play music in the background and rarely look at the lyrics, but this time the melody made me look them up. I still couldn't place it. Then I checked the comments and saw someone say it was the closing theme from the TV drama *鍋居*. All at once, memories I'd locked away somewhere deep in my mind came rushing back.

The song left me with a mix of calm, helplessness, and sadness. Once I knew what song it was, I understood why it made me feel that way. The drama came out around 2009, and I watched it around 2010, when I was just starting my working life. The global financial crisis was still recent, and economies around the world were recovering. I had a girlfriend at the time. I earned more than she did, and her income was low and unstable. I did a lot to help her, but things didn't end well. We ended up breaking up.

It was much like the ending of the drama. So much time has passed that I no longer remember the details of the breakup, but the feeling was much the same as in the show. The longing and the memories stayed with me, even somewhere below the surface of my awareness. That's why I was surprised by how much I felt when I heard the song again.

It's been a long time, and I have a happy family now, so the song feels different. It no longer brings me to tears as soon as I hear it. What I feel now is some helplessness, some sadness, and the calm that comes with letting go. That breakup helped me grow a great deal emotionally.

The pain of losing someone took me from being an immature boy to becoming a man. Around then, I started making a real effort to learn about relationships between men and women. I realized that what I thought I knew about relationships was all in my head. Real life was different. I'll write about the details another time if I get the chance.

Back to the song. It's like a switch. It unlocks my memories of that time, and in an instant I'm back there, a young and inexperienced boy again.

Every song that has stayed with me holds a different memory, with its own people, moments, and things. Now that I'm older, I can step back and see the bigger picture when I watch people struggle with love. I know how to let go. That's the price of love, and the price of growing up.
